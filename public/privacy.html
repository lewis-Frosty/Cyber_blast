Privacy Policy

Cyber Blast · Last updated 10 September 2026

Cyber Blast is a puzzle game. This policy explains exactly what it collects, why, and what it does not. It is written to be checked against the app's actual behaviour rather than to cover every theoretical possibility.

The short version. You do not create an account, give an email address, or sign in. The game gives your device an anonymous ID so your scores follow you. It stores a display name you choose, an avatar, a country, and your game results. It has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

What we collect

Account

The first time you play, the game creates an anonymous account through our backend provider, Supabase. This is a random identifier. It is not linked to your email address, phone number, Google account, name, or any advertising identifier. We never ask for and never receive those.

Profile you choose

DataWhy
Display name (up to 12 characters)Shown next to your score on leaderboards. Defaults to “ANON”. Pick anything; it does not have to be your real name, and we would rather it were not.
AvatarOne of twelve built-in images. No photo upload exists in the app.
Country (two-letter code)Powers the country leaderboard. Guessed from your device's language setting — not from GPS, not from your IP address — and you can change or clear it in the Profile screen.

Game results

  • Scores, number of pieces placed, longest chain, and how long a game lasted
  • Which mode you played and, for a Daily Challenge, which date
  • Totals: games played, best score, best chain, daily streak
  • In-game XP and currency, and your league group and points

Anti-cheat, and what happens to it

When a game ends, the app sends the sequence of moves you made to our server, which replays that game to work out your score. This is why a score cannot be faked: the number is calculated by us, never claimed by the app.

The move list is not stored. It is used to compute the score and then discarded. Only the resulting numbers are kept.

Technical data our provider records

Like any internet service, our backend provider (Supabase) records standard server logs when the app talks to it. These include your IP address and browser/device user-agent string, and are retained for a limited period for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them to build a profile of you and do not combine them with your game data.

What we do not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or real name
  • No password — there is nothing to sign in to
  • No precise or GPS location
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising ID, and no advertising or analytics SDK of any kind
  • No payment information — the game is free and contains no purchases
If this changes, this page changes first. The launch plan includes an optional paid “remove ads” purchase and, with it, advertising. Neither exists in the app today. Nothing will be added that collects more data without this policy being updated and the Google Play Data safety section updated to match.

Stored on your device only

Some things never leave your phone. The app keeps them in local storage so it works offline and remembers your preferences:

  • Your sign-in session for the anonymous account
  • Your name, avatar and country, so they show instantly before the network replies
  • Colour-blind glyph mode and sound on/off
  • Whether you have seen the how-to-play screen
  • Any finished game that could not be submitted because you were offline, held for up to two hours and then discarded

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes all of this. Note that it also removes your anonymous account key: without it there is no way to prove a past account was yours, so your scores and progress cannot be recovered.

Who can see your data

  • Other players see your display name, avatar, country and score on leaderboards and in your league group. Nothing else about you is visible to them.
  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and server functions as our processor. Their privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Vercel hosts the web version of the game: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone for their own purposes.

Where your data is held

Our database is hosted by Supabase and the data may be stored and processed outside your country. Where the law requires a transfer safeguard, we rely on our provider's standard contractual protections.

How long we keep it

  • Profile and game results — until you ask us to delete them.
  • Move lists — not kept at all, as above.
  • Unfinished games — expired automatically; abandoned ones are cleared when you next start a game.
  • Server logs — kept by our provider for a limited period on their standard retention schedule.

Your rights, and how to delete your data

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. You can change your display name, avatar and country yourself at any time in the Profile screen.

To have your account and all associated data deleted, email PLACEHOLDER — your contact email from the device you play on, and include your display name and country. We will confirm and delete within 30 days. Deletion removes your profile, statistics, wallet, league membership and all recorded games. It cannot be undone.

If you are in the EEA or UK you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in New Zealand you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Children

Cyber Blast is not directed at children under 13. It has no chat, no messaging, no friend requests, no user-uploaded images and no purchases. The only thing a player can type is a 12-character display name, which is visible to other players — please do not enter a real name or anything identifying.

Security

Every score is calculated by our server; the app cannot submit a number of its own. Access to the database is restricted per player by row-level security, so one player cannot read or change another's data. Sensitive operations run only on the server.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, note it in the app's release notes.
